About Linking You

Connecting People with Opportunities

Who We Are

Linking You is a career and recruitment platform created to connect job seekers with companies looking for talented and motivated employees.


We make the job-search process easier by bringing job opportunities, companies and useful career resources together in one convenient platform.

Our Mission

Our mission is to connect the right people with the right opportunities. We help job seekers discover suitable employment opportunities while helping companies find potential employees who can contribute to their success.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted platform that makes employment opportunities more accessible and creates stronger connections between job seekers and employers.
