Job Recommendations For You

Find useful job opportunities, career advice and practical tips to help you improve your chances of getting hired.

Connect • Empowerment • Growth

1. Job Hunting Tips

  • Apply fast: Applying soon after a job is posted can improve your chances.
  • Use multiple platforms: Indeed, Careers24 and LinkedIn.
  • Location matters: CV must show your location.
  • Keywords matter: Include "data capture", "MS Excel" or "filing".
  • Use a professional file name: Name_Surname_Admin_CV.pdf
  • Follow up: Politely express interest after applying.

2. CV Writing: Private vs Public Posts

TypeWhat it meansCV changes
Private CompanyRetail, banking, telcosConcise, achievements, action words
Government / PublicDepartments, municipalitiesFollow vacancy requirements
NGO / NPOSocial/communityHighlight volunteering

3. Internship vs Apprenticeship vs Learnership vs Junior Role

TypeFor who?QualificationOutputPay
InternshipGraduatesDiploma/DegreeExperienceStipend
ApprenticeshipTradesTrade depCertificationAllowance
LearnershipStructuredVariesQualificationStipend
Junior PositionStartingDependsEmploymentSalary
Important: Always read vacancy requirements before applying.

4. Interview Tips That Work

  1. Before: Research company
  2. Dress: Professional
  3. STAR: Situation, Task, Action, Result
  4. Weakness: Genuine + improvement
  5. Ask: What does success look like in 6 months?
  6. After: Thank-you message
Final checklist:

1. CV matches requirements?
2. Keywords included?
3. Professional email?
4. Spelling checked?
5. Correct documents attached?
