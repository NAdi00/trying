Job Hunting & CV Guide for South Africa

1. Job Hunting Tips

  • Apply fast: First 48 hours after posting gets 80% of recruiter attention
  • Use 3 platforms: Indeed + Careers24 + LinkedIn covers 90% of SA jobs
  • Location filter: Set your profile to the job suburb. Recruiters filter by distance first
  • Keywords matter: Copy 5-6 exact words from the job post into your CV: "data capture", "MS Excel", "filing"
  • File name: Save as Name_Surname_Admin_CV.pdf
  • Follow up: No reply after 5 days? Call HR: "Hi, I applied for X role and I'm very keen"

2. CV Writing: Private vs Public Posts

Type What it means CV changes to make
Private Company Business: Clicks, Shoprite, Discovery. Fast hiring Short 1-2 pages. Focus on skills + profit. Use action words: "Increased", "Saved", "Processed"
Government / Public Dept of Health, Municipality, SAPS. Z83 form needed Longer 3-4 pages. Full details. Attach certified copies. Use exact wording from advert
NGO / NPO Harambee, Gift of the Givers. Mission-driven Add volunteer work. Show "passion" and "community". Values matter more than profit

3. Internship vs Apprenticeship vs Learnership vs Junior Role

Type For who? Qualification Output Pay
Internship Graduates with diploma/degree You MUST have tertiary qual Work experience only Stipend R4000-R8000
Apprenticeship Future artisans: plumber, electrician Grade 9-12. Tech college helps Trade Test + become artisan Increases yearly R3000+
Learnership Unemployed youth 18-35 Matric ONLY. Degree disqualifies you NQF Level 3-5 certificate Stipend R2000-R6000
Junior Position Anyone starting career Matric or diploma depending on job Permanent job Salary R7000+
Important: For learnerships, remove your degree from CV. They target people with NO tertiary education. Keep it to Matric only.

4. Interview Tips That Work

  1. Before: Research company. Know 3 facts: what they do, CEO name, recent news
  2. Dress: Even for warehouse jobs, wear clean pants + collared shirt. First impression = hired
  3. STAR method: When asked "Tell me about a time...", answer: Situation → Task → Action → Result
  4. Weakness question: Say a real weakness + how you fix it: "I'm shy, so I prepare my points before meetings"
  5. Your questions: Always ask 2: "What does success look like in 6 months?" and "What training do you provide?"
  6. After: Send SMS/Email same day: "Thank you for your time today. I'm very excited about the opportunity to join your team."
Final checklist before you send any application:
1. Does my CV location match the job suburb? 2. Did I use 5+ keywords from the advert? 3. Is my email subject correct? 4. Did I remove degree if it's a learnership?
